MEGHALAYA TRAVEL GUIDE

Meghalaya is a beautiful state in Northeast India, known for its
waterfalls, caves, green landscapes and living root bridges.

Popular Destinations:
Shillong is the capital of Meghalaya and a popular base for travellers.
Cherrapunji is famous for waterfalls, caves and heavy rainfall.
Dawki is known for the clear waters of the Umngot River.
Mawlynnong is known for its clean village environment.
Nongriat is famous for the Double Decker Living Root Bridge.

Popular Attractions:
Nohkalikai Falls is one of the tallest plunge waterfalls in India.
Mawsmai Cave is a popular limestone cave near Cherrapunji.
The Double Decker Living Root Bridge is a famous natural attraction
that requires a trek to reach.

Best Time to Visit:
October to April is generally a good period to explore Meghalaya.
The monsoon season usually brings heavy rainfall and can affect
trekking and road travel.

Food:
Popular local dishes include Jadoh, Dohneiiong and Tungrymbai.

Budget:
A budget traveller may spend approximately Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 3,000
per person per day, excluding flights.
