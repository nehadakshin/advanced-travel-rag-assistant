GOA TRAVEL GUIDE

Goa is a popular tourist destination in India, known for its beaches,
Portuguese architecture, seafood and nightlife.

Popular Beaches:
Baga Beach is known for nightlife, water sports and beach shacks.
Calangute Beach is one of the busiest beaches in North Goa.
Palolem Beach in South Goa is known for its scenic coastline and
relatively peaceful atmosphere.

Popular Attractions:
Fort Aguada is a historic Portuguese fort overlooking the Arabian Sea.
Basilica of Bom Jesus in Old Goa is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
Dudhsagar Falls is a famous waterfall located on the Goa-Karnataka
border.

Best Time to Visit:
November to February is generally considered the best time to visit Goa.
The weather is relatively pleasant during these months.
June to September is the monsoon season.

Food:
Goan cuisine includes fish curry rice, prawn balchao, bebinca and
other seafood dishes.

Budget:
A budget traveller may spend approximately Rs. 1,500 to Rs. 2,500
per person per day, excluding flights.
